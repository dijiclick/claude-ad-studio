# Hook library (from the course's winning prompts + member call)

The first 2–3 s decide the ad. The same body with a different hook swings cost per result 2–4×, so every ad ships with **5 hook variants** on one shared body (change only the first line + first gesture).

## Lead types (pick one per ad — Module 2)
| Lead | What it does | When |
|---|---|---|
| Problem-Solution | names a relatable pain, then the fix | default for utility products |
| Secret / forbidden knowledge | "they won't tell you this" | curiosity, cheap attention |
| Promise | one bold, *true* outcome | clear, provable benefit |
| Story | mini emotional arc (call with mum, day in the life) | warm products, older audiences |
| Objection | quotes the doubt, then answers it | price-sensitive offers |
| Live proof | reaction while trying it on camera | visible, instant results |

**Awareness → lead** (Great Leads): most aware → Offer · product-aware → Promise · problem-aware → Problem-Solution · unaware/skeptical → Secret, Proclamation, Story. Flag the buyer, not everyone; one idea per ad; specific numbers beat superlatives.

## 12 hook patterns (verbatim course examples → reusable pattern)
Each hook = **line + first gesture**. Keep ≤ 8 words for the first beat; the gesture lands on the key word.

1. **Quoted objection** — "'Too expensive.' That's what everyone says about this protein." → raises eyebrows on "expensive".
   Pattern: `"'[the doubt].' [Everyone/Herkes] bunu söylüyor."`
2. **Forbidden knowledge** — "My parents are neurologists and they're probably gonna hate me for telling you this…" → leans in.
   Pattern: `"[Insiders] sana bunu söylemez ama…"`
3. **Myth-bust / contrarian** — "I'm about to ruin bananas for you forever." → holds the object up.
   Pattern: `"[Common habit]'i bırak. Çünkü…"`
4. **Live first try** — "This is literally real time me trying it… Holy— look at that." → reveal to camera.
   Pattern: react to the product/phone mid-sentence, then show it.
5. **Gifted skeptic** — "When they reached out to me I was a little skeptical at first…" → (testimony — only where allowed).
6. **Anti-influencer** — "I'm not gonna hit you with some typical TikTok Shop scripted post." → dismissive hand wave.
7. **Credibility number** — "292 days…" (a real, checkable number) → counts on fingers / points at screen.
8. **Bold prediction** — "You're not going to hit your goals unless… I'm predicting right now…" → points at lens.
9. **Vulnerable cliffhanger** — "…and it all came down to…" (cut) → looks away, then back.
10. **Symptom stack question** — "Bloated, tired, foggy? That is until I fixed this one thing." → counts symptoms.
11. **Visual A/B** — "Do you want this… or this?" → swaps two props.
12. **Routine reveal** — "This is how I start my mornings now." → mid-action (sipping, eating).

"Create a spark" (member call): don't say *a dog sits on a mat*; say *a dog sits on the cat's mat* — one unexpected detail makes the line stick.

## Presenter-safe versions (when the AI person may not testify)
Many markets/brands (e.g. Türkiye ad rules since 1 Aug 2026, anyone labelling AI characters) forbid an AI person claiming personal use. Use patterns 1–4, 7, 8, 11 as a **presenter/explainer**, never 5, 6, 12 in first person:
- Objection: "'Bir uygulama daha mı?' Önce şunu dinle."
- Secret: "Mağazaların sana söylemediği bir şey var."
- Myth-bust: "İndirimi bekleyip durmayı bırak."
- Live reaction (to real data on a real screen): "Dur… bu 2.699'du?"
- Credibility number (real, checked today): "18 markanın indirimi tek yerde."
- Visual A/B: "Bunu mu istersin… yoksa bunu mu?"
(Draft examples — the project's copy process has the final word on wording.)

## Rules
- Hook first, never the brand name first; no pitch in the first line.
- Real numbers only; no speed promises you can't keep.
- 5 hooks share one body → cheap to produce, and the test data tells you which lead type to scale.
