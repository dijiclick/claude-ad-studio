# Advertorial Swipe File: Patterns

The Drive folder has 21 PDFs, which come to 19 unique advertorials (Produce Pro is duplicated, and "Reasons Why Resilia" is a partial copy of `try_resilia`). One analysis file per advertorial is in this folder.

## Common skeleton
1. **Wrapper:** "Advertorial" label + fake publication chrome (masthead, byline, "X hours ago", view/share counts)
2. **Hook headline:** authority + reveal + curiosity ("Harvard Dermatologist Reveals... (Not Collagen Loss)")
3. **Call-out + agitation:** "If you have X...", symptom list, emotional/social cost
4. **Failed alternatives / enemy:** why creams, pills, gyms or "Big Pharma" haven't worked
5. **New mechanism:** the "real" root cause, plus one analogy that makes it simple
6. **Discovery story:** expert or narrator finds the solution (often by accident or during late-night research)
7. **Product reveal + how it works + how to use** (in 3 steps)
8. **Proof stack:** testimonials, stats, "as seen on", references, rating block
9. **Offer:** anchor price → discount → per-day cost → bundle logic → guarantee
10. **Urgency + CTA:** stock/time limit, repeated buttons, then a Facebook-style comment wall

Short "bridge" pages cover only steps 1–5 and send the reader to a video (VSL) or quiz. Long pages run all ten, often twice.

## Length → when it's used
| Length | Words | Used for | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short (bridge) | 180–550 | Pre-sell cold native traffic into a VSL or quiz: sell the click, not the product | ED, eye bag, bladder, fat burn, dad bod, crepey skin |
| Medium | 600–1,800 | Simple or low-price products, lead-gen, and offers where the reader already knows the category (hearing aids, showers, Rx telehealth) | Oricle, HomeBuddy, Friday Plans, cardiologist, tinnitus, Produce Pro, Resilia, PrimeValueVision |
| Long | 1,400–3,900 | Skeptical or high-emotion buyers who need a new mechanism, a full story and heavy proof before a direct checkout | Vibriance, Luxe, Nuzzle, MediLisk hearing, CellTox |

## Don't copy (deceptive devices found in these files)
- **Fake news wrappers:** mastheads like "Urology Research Group News" or "Health Headlines", "Breaking Report", fresh timestamps, fake view/share counters, and CNBC-style datelines.
- **Invented experts:** "Dr. Tessa Morgan", "Dr. John Martin", "Dr. Hargrove", "Dr. Reyes", "Dr. Rawlings" and "Dr. J" are unverifiable personas. Headlines promise "Cardiologists" or "Harvard" and the body never delivers them. One "audiologist" name even changes spelling partway through the page.
- **Fabricated social proof:** the same comment cast (Skyler Greig, Marie Cambell, Debra Peyton, "I bought mine at full price...") is pasted into the Tinnitus, Hearing Spray and Resilia pages. An identical "3,791 ratings, 90/7/2/0/1 %" block appears on CellTox, Resilia and Hearing Spray. Doctors show up as "Verified Buyers", and footers admit "testimonials may be real or fictitious" and "photos are models".
- **Unprovable or dangerous health claims:** ivermectin + fenbendazole sold for cancer; "restore 100% of hearing"; sprays that treat otitis media; ED gone in 30 minutes; fat loss "without diet or exercise"; self-diagnosing parasites.
- **Fake government programs:** a "Hearing Accessibility Program" that makes the aid $99, and an "FHA Cash-Out Plan" that "Congress will end".
- **Fake scarcity and suppression:** "first 200 readers", "if you're reading this we still have stock", "before it's taken down", "cease-and-desist any day", "Big Pharma lobbying", auto-dated "UPDATE: selling out" banners.
- **Bait-and-switch:** a "method" or "free tutorial" that turns out to be a supplement, and a "we tested 17 creams" headline with no comparison shown.
- **Misused science:** real in-vitro papers presented as clinical proof, a nutrition study stretched to "organic is pointless", and "clinically proven" with no citation.
- **Guilt and fear closes:** "your partner will have to get used to living alone", and "bad pillow → irreversible nerve damage".
- **Template leftovers show this is mass-produced:** wrong product names (IverPure/CellTox, Soursop Bitters on the Resilia page), "garlic oil" in a hearing-spray disclaimer, "pouches" for softgels, and a stray "4o" in one page's disclaimer.

**Rule of thumb for our own work:** keep the structure (hook → mechanism → proof → offer → CTA), and make every person, number, study and deadline real. Label the page as an ad and don't dress it up as a news site.
