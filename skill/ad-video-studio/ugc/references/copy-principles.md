# Copy Principles Cheat Sheet (merged from the 5 course books)
Sources: GL = Great Leads (Masterson) · OG = Ogilvy on Advertising · RW = Reason Why Advertising (Kennedy) · SA = Scientific Advertising (Hopkins) · WU = Wundt (weak, analogy only)

## 0. Before writing
- Advertising is a salesperson multiplied. Cut anything a good salesperson wouldn't say face to face. [RW, SA, OG]
- Do your homework: take the customer's exact words from reviews and comments, and find the fact nobody else has told. [OG, SA]
- Tag the viewer's awareness level (most aware → product → solution → problem → unaware). It decides the lead type. [GL]
- One ad = one big idea, one emotion, one promise, one CTA. Three benefits means three ads. [GL, OG]
- Choose the appeal by testing, not taste. Angles can differ 5-10x (beauty vs health vs savings). [SA, OG]

## 1. Hook (first 1-3 s + first line + on-screen text)
- The hook is most of the ad. Write it first and test many hooks per body. [GL, SA]
- Its job is to flag the buyer, not everyone ("If you own a dog that sheds..."). Wrong-audience attention is wasted. [SA, RW]
- Pick the lead by awareness level, from direct to indirect:
  - Offer: most aware
  - Promise: product-aware
  - Problem-Solution: problem-aware
  - Secret / Proclamation / Story: unaware or skeptical [GL]
- Put a benefit or news in the hook. Benefit headlines are read about 4x more, news gets about 22% more recall. [OG]
- Be specific: numbers, times, counts. Superlatives get discounted. [SA, OG]
- Curiosity: a strange true fact, or a secret teased but not revealed until the midpoint. [SA, GL]
- A question only works if the viewer instantly thinks "yes". [GL]
- If a claim is tired, admit the doubt ("I didn't believe it either"). [GL]
- First frame: one clear focal subject, low clutter, contrast with the feed. Clarity beats loudness. [OG, WU]
- No puns, blind hooks or visual clichés. Humor only if the joke is the benefit. [OG, SA]

## 2. Script (15 s vertical)
- Default beat sheet:
  - 0-3s: hook (lead type)
  - 3-7s: one reason why (mechanism, ingredient, number)
  - 7-11s: proof (demo, before/after, result, testimonial)
  - 11-15s: offer + CTA
  [RW, SA, OG, GL]
- Problem-Solution: stay on the pain just long enough (no more than ~1/3 of the ad), then pivot to hope and show the good end state. [GL, SA]
- Always give a reason why. For deals, say why the deal exists. [RW, GL]
- Show the product in use and its visible result. The product is the hero. [OG, SA]
- Tell a complete story in one exposure. Assume zero brand knowledge, and don't rely on "part 2". [SA]
- Tell viewers what they'll notice ("cools on contact"). It primes the experience and repeat purchase. [RW, SA]
- Arm them against substitutes: say what makes *this* one different, so they search the brand, not the category. [RW, SA]
- Real user on camera beats VO and celebrities. Match persona, slang and setting to the buyer (the "responsive chord"). [OG, RW]
- Say the brand/product early (~3s) and again at the end. Put the key claim on screen as text when it's spoken. [OG]
- Give the viewer a positive CTA ("grab yours"), not guilt. Add a reason to act now (deadline, limited, free trial). [SA, GL]
- Cure beats prevention. Frame as fixing a current pain or gaining something visible. [SA]
- Repeat the core cue 2-3x inside the ad. Don't let fast cuts make the claim unreadable. [OG, WU]

## 3. Static ad
- Image first, headline under it, then short copy (the natural reading order). [OG]
- About 5x more people read the headline than the body: benefit/news + brand + audience flag, specific number. [OG]
- Before/after or product-in-use image. Every visual must sell or it's cut. [OG, SA]
- Native/editorial look (post, note, article) beats an obvious ad. [OG]
- Readable type: no all-caps blocks, no reversed body text. [OG]
- Testimonial or specific proof in the copy. Guarantee or trial shown clearly. [OG, GL, SA]

## 4. After launch
- Judge on cost per result, never on opinion. Taste-picked copy has lost to tested copy by 3-35x. [RW, SA]
- A/B the same body with different hooks, keep everything else equal, then cross-check the winner in a second set. [RW, SA]
- Test lead *types* against each other. The winner reveals the audience's awareness level. [GL]
- Keep a winner running and iterate around it. Rotate hooks before fatigue. [RW, SA, OG, WU]
